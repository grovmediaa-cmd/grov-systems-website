GROV SYSTEMS
Your 5-minute diagnostic

Start with the relationship, not the reach.

Check what is already true in your business. The gaps will point you toward the opportunity that deserves attention.

WHO

☐ I can describe my buyer's current problem in their own words.
☐ I understand what they've already tried.
☐ I know what they want next.

WHERE

☐ I know where they discover people like me.
☐ I know where they validate a provider.
☐ I know where deeper conversations happen.

TRUST

☐ I demonstrate that I understand the problem.
☐ I communicate a clear point of view.
☐ I show credible proof.
☐ People understand what working with me feels like.

PLATFORM

☐ Each platform has a clear job.
☐ My messaging feels connected across channels.
☐ I don't expect every platform to create the same action.

CONVERSION

☐ The next step is obvious.
☐ It feels like a natural continuation of the relationship.
☐ My offer connects to the problem I've been talking about.
☐ Buyers understand why they should act.

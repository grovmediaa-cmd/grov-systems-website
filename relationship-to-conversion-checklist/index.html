Before you pitch

A quick positioning check

If people are interested but not buying, the gap may not be the platform. It may be positioning.

Relationship gets attention to lean in. Identity is what makes it convert.

TURN THE CHECKLIST INTO ACTION

What to do with what you found

You do not need to fix every platform. Use your answers to decide what deserves attention first.

01

Find the friction

Look for the one place where attention stops becoming trust: buyer clarity, platform role, messaging, or your next step.

02

Give one platform a job

Choose the platform closest to your buyer and decide whether its job is discovery, trust, depth, community, or decision.

03

Build one bridge

Create one clear next step that moves someone from seeing you to knowing you, trusting you, and eventually choosing you.

The goal isn't to be everywhere. It's to make the journey between being seen and being chosen make sense.
